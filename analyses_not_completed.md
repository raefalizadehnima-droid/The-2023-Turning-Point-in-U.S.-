# Analyses not completed or not supported by the data

| Analysis | Reason |
|---|---|
| Preliminary vs final observations (RQ5) | Only one data vintage per source was supplied. |
| Reporting-delay estimation | No report-date or vintage fields; delay was represented by scenario lags L = 0, 3, 6 months. |
| Population-adjusted cohort death rates | Published rates are age-adjusted per jurisdiction; cohort population denominators are not in the SUDORS file. Counts and shares used instead. |
| Case-level co-occurrence networks | Data are aggregate; no individual records exist. Only published pairwise combination counts were screened. |
| Drug-specific predictive models (fentanyl, heroin, cocaine, methamphetamine, benzodiazepine) | DOSE-SYS indicators for these drugs are suppressed/unreported for most jurisdiction-months (3-10 complete jurisdictions). |
| 41 of 108 SUDORS categories in the share screening | Fewer than 10 jurisdictions with valid counts in all five years, or fewer than 100 deaths in 2024 (mostly nitazene/MDMA/cathinone combinations). |
| DOSE race/ethnicity, sex/age and county sheets | Not needed for the research questions; not analysed (documented in the inventory). |
| Seasonality estimation beyond one harmonic | Only 60 monthly fatal observations per series. |
| Verification of JCR quartiles | JCR is not directly accessible from this environment; see journal_shortlist.md. |
